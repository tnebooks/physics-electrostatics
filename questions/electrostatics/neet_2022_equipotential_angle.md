## The angle between the electric lines of force and the equipotential surface is:

*(NEET 2022 Phase 1)*

#### On an equipotential surface dV = 0. Since dV = −E·dr·cosθ, cosθ must be 0, giving θ = 90°. Electric field lines are always perpendicular to equipotential surfaces.
