## An electric dipole with dipole moment $5 \times 10^{-6}$ C·m is aligned with the direction of a uniform electric field of magnitude $4 \times 10^5$ N/C. The dipole is then rotated through an angle of $60°$ with respect to the electric field. The change in the potential energy of the dipole is:

*(NEET 2025)*

#### ΔU = pE(cos θᵢ − cos θ_f) = 5×10⁻⁶ × 4×10⁵ × (cos 0° − cos 60°) = 2 × (1 − 0.5) = 1.0 J.
